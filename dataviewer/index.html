Laboratory test procedure guide

Open full laboratory test procedure

  1. Record the reference length L0 after 15 min at Tref.
  2. Virgin curve: load from Tref to Tdesign.
  3. Design hold: hold at Tdesign for 15 min.
  4. First unloading: unload from Tdesign to Tref.
  5. Reference hold: hold at Tref for 5 min; record L5.
  6. Dynamic loading: reload and cycle between Tref and Tdesign for 20 cycles. The final loading branch is the last dynamic load cycle.

Stage targets default to Results Fmax for T_design and 1% of (Fmax ? 2.3) for T_ref. Both force values remain editable. Check estimated ranges against the test record before interpreting them.
