Laboratory procedure

Rope and Netting Test

Characterisation of aquaculture ropes and netting for AquaSim modelling

This procedure characterises tensile load–strain behaviour for engineering analysis. It records the virgin load–strain curve, permanent creep (construction strain or set), and dynamic load–strain curve. The dynamic curve is intended as the primary nonlinear constitutive relationship in AquaSim.

PurposeSpecimensPre-test informationPreparationTest procedure

1. Purpose

Determine the tensile load–strain behaviour of aquaculture ropes and netting intended for engineering analysis and AquaSim modelling.

  • Virgin load–strain curve
  • Permanent creep (construction strain or set)
  • Dynamic load–strain curve

3. Pre-test information

Record the following before testing:

  • Specimen ID, material, and construction type
  • Nominal rope diameter, or netting mesh size and thread specification
  • Wet or dry condition
  • Water temperature (10 °C default) and room temperature (20 °C default)
  • Manufacturer’s Minimum Breaking Strength (MBS)

Design load

For rope:

Tdesign = MBS / 2.3

For netting:

Tdesign = MBS / (2 × 2.3)

This corresponds approximately to the maximum design load used in NS 9415 structural assessments.

Reference load

For rope:

Tref = 1% × MBS

For netting:

Tref = 0.5 × 1% × MBS

4. Specimen preparation

  1. Immerse specimens in tap water for a minimum of 24 hours.
  2. Install specimens wet whenever practical.
  3. Inspect each specimen for visible damage before testing.

5. Test procedure

Step 1 — Install the specimen

  • Mount the specimen in the test machine.
  • Carefully align the loading axis.
  • Install the displacement reflex tape.

Step 2 — Establish the initial reference length, L0

  1. Apply the reference load, Tref.
  2. Hold for 15 minutes.
  3. Measure and record the initial length L0.

Step 3 — Measure the virgin curve

  1. Load continuously from Tref to Tdesign.
  2. Use a loading rate corresponding to approximately 1% strain per minute.

Step 4 — Conditioning hold

  1. Maintain the load at Tdesign.
  2. Hold for 15 minutes.

Step 5 — Unloading

  1. Unload continuously from Tdesign to Tref.
  2. Use the same rate as during loading.

Step 6 — Measure permanent creep

  1. Hold at Tref for 5 minutes.
  2. Record the length at the end of the hold as L5.
  3. Calculate permanent creep:
Permanent creep = (L5 - L0) / L0

For ropes, this parameter represents construction strain. For netting, it represents permanent set from mesh reorientation, knot tightening, and structural settlement.

Step 7 — Measure the dynamic load–strain curve

  1. Reload continuously from Tref to Tdesign.
  2. Use the same loading rate as in Step 3 and force-control speed for 20 cycles unless the test plan specifies otherwise.
  3. Record load and elongation continuously throughout the test.
